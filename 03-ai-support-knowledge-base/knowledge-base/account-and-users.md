# Account and user management

## Roles
- **Owner**: full control, including billing and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has exactly one Owner.
- **Admin**: manages members, projects and integrations, but cannot change billing or delete the workspace.
- **Member**: creates and edits projects they have access to.
- **Guest**: limited to the projects they are invited to; free on all plans.

## Transfer ownership
The current Owner can transfer ownership to an Admin in **Settings > Members**, by opening the Admin's menu and choosing **Make owner**. The previous Owner becomes an Admin.

## Remove a member
Admins and the Owner can remove members in **Settings > Members**. Tasks assigned to a removed member stay in the project and become unassigned. On paid plans the seat is freed and credited at the next billing cycle.

## Change your email address
Go to **Profile > Account > Email**, enter the new address and confirm it with the link we send. If your workspace uses SSO, the email is managed by your identity provider.

## Delete the workspace
Only the Owner can delete a workspace, in **Settings > Workspace > Delete workspace**. Data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind and then permanently erased. Deleting a workspace also cancels any paid subscription.
