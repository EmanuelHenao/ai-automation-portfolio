# Refunds and cancellations

## How to cancel
The workspace Owner can cancel a paid plan at any time in **Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription**. The workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the period that has already been paid, then moves to the Free plan.

## Refund policy
- **Annual plans**: you can request a full refund within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date.
- **Monthly plans**: monthly payments are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time to avoid the next charge.
- Prorated charges for added seats are not refundable.

Refunds must be requested by the workspace Owner by contacting support. They are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If you believe you were charged incorrectly or twice, contact support with the invoice number. Billing disputes are always reviewed by our billing team; the support assistant cannot issue refunds or credits.

## What happens to your data
After a workspace is moved to the Free plan, projects above the Free limit become read-only but are not deleted. If you delete the workspace instead, data is kept for 30 days and then permanently erased.
