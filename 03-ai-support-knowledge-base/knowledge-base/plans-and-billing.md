# Plans and billing

## Plans
- **Free**: up to 3 members and 5 active projects, 1 GB of storage, 7-day activity history.
- **Pro**: USD 12 per member per month (USD 9.60 billed annually). Unlimited projects, 100 GB storage, timelines, workload view, integrations and API access.
- **Business**: USD 24 per member per month (USD 19.20 billed annually). Everything in Pro plus SAML single sign-on (SSO), EU data residency, audit log, advanced permissions and priority support with a 4-hour response time.

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Pro and Business. Guests are free on every plan.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express on all paid plans. Business customers on annual billing can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice (net 30). PayPal is not supported.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. When you add members in the middle of a cycle, the extra seats are prorated.

## Invoices and taxes
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are available in **Settings > Billing > Invoices**. You can add a company name, address and VAT/tax ID to your invoices. Sales tax or VAT is added where required by law.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails we retry it 3 times over 7 days and email the billing contact each time. After that the workspace is moved to the Free plan; no data is deleted.
