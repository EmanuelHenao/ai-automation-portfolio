# Security and privacy

## Certifications
TaskPilot is SOC 2 Type II certified and an annual penetration test is performed by an independent firm. The latest report is available to Business customers under NDA.

## Where data is stored
Data is hosted on AWS. By default workspaces are stored in the United States (us-east-1). Business plan customers can choose **EU data residency** (Frankfurt, eu-central-1) when the workspace is created; existing workspaces can be migrated by contacting support.

## Encryption
All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 30 days.

## Authentication
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) with an authenticator app is available on all plans, and Admins can make it mandatory.
- **Single sign-on (SSO)** with SAML 2.0 (Okta, Azure AD / Entra ID, Google Workspace) is available on the **Business** plan only.

## Privacy and GDPR
TaskPilot is GDPR compliant and offers a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which can be signed in **Settings > Billing > Legal**. We never sell customer data or use it to train AI models.

## Reporting a security issue
Report vulnerabilities to security@taskpilot.example. If you suspect your account was compromised, contact support immediately so the team can lock the account and review the audit log.
