# Getting started with TaskPilot

TaskPilot is a project management tool for small and mid-sized teams: boards, lists, timelines and workload views in one workspace.

## Create your workspace
Sign up at app.taskpilot.example with your work email or Google account. The person who creates the workspace becomes its **Owner**. You can rename the workspace and upload a logo in **Settings > Workspace > General**.

## Invite your team
Go to **Settings > Members > Invite** and enter one or more email addresses, or share an invite link. Invites expire after 7 days. You choose a role for each person when inviting them (Admin, Member or Guest). Guests can only see the projects they are explicitly added to and do not count towards paid seats.

## Create your first project
Click **New project**, choose a template (Kanban, Sprint, Content calendar or Blank) and add tasks. Every task can have an assignee, due date, priority, labels, subtasks, comments and file attachments up to 100 MB each.

## Import from other tools
You can import projects from Trello, Asana and Jira, or from a CSV file, in **Settings > Import**. Trello and Asana imports keep comments and attachments; CSV imports only bring task fields. See the troubleshooting guide if a CSV import fails.

## Mobile and desktop apps
TaskPilot has apps for iOS, Android, macOS and Windows. All apps sync in real time with the web version and support offline mode for viewing tasks.
